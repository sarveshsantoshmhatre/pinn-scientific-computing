THE BIG IDEA

What is a PINN?

AI

Neural network

A small deep-learning model receives position and time, then predicts the temperature at that point.

Σ

Physics knowledge

The governing heat equation becomes part of the training objective. The network is rewarded for obeying it.

✓

Scientific validation

The learned solution is compared with an analytical solution and a traditional finite-difference method.

“

In simple words

Imagine asking an AI to predict the temperature along a metal rod. A normal neural network learns patterns from examples. A PINN also knows the rule that heat must follow, so it learns a solution that is both accurate and physically consistent.
